        Tips: chords and timing

        Put chords in square brackets just before the word they land on, like [Am]Hello [F]there. You can also paste a chord sheet with chords on the line above the words.

        Lines starting with a time like [01:23.45] are synced and scroll exactly on time. You can make these yourself with Tap to time in the player.
